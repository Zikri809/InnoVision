# Troubleshooting

Use the closest symptom below. Keep the quiz page open during an assessment problem when possible. For issues that need staff access, contact your lecturer first; see [Support](support.md) for what to include and where to escalate.

## Account and sign-in

### I cannot create an account

Check that **Email** is valid, the password has at least six characters, a student role is selected, and the requested **Matric number** is filled in. The registration form requires the biometric consent checkbox before **Create account** is enabled. Read the consent information before selecting it. If a field is rejected, correct it and submit again. If the same error persists, contact your institution's account administrator.

### The app says the email or password is invalid

Check the spelling and sign-in method. If you use institutional Microsoft sign-in, choose that option when it is offered. Select **Forgot password?** to request a reset link, then check the email inbox and spam folder. The reset confirmation is generic by design. If you cannot access your email or the reset link has expired, contact the institution's account administrator.

### I am asked for a matric number

Complete the one-time matric capture prompt for your institutional account. If the value is rejected, check the institution's expected format and contact its account administrator. Do not create a second account to work around the prompt.

### A QR join link sends me to sign in and I do not return to the class

Finish sign-in or registration in the same browser. Open the QR link again if necessary, review the displayed class code, then explicitly confirm the join. A QR scan only opens the confirmation page; it does not enroll you automatically.

## Class joining

### The join code is rejected

Check that it has six characters and compare it with the lecturer's current code. Codes can be invalid or for an archived class. If several incorrect attempts have triggered a temporary lock, wait for the lock to expire and ask the lecturer to confirm the code. Do not keep guessing.

### The app says I already joined

Open **My Classes** and look for the class. Select its card to view quizzes. If it is not present after refreshing the page, sign out and back in once; then contact the lecturer with the class name.

### I joined, but cannot find a quiz

Open **Class Quizzes**, clear a class filter if one is active, and confirm the quiz is live and within its availability window. Draft quizzes are not visible to students. Ask the lecturer whether the quiz has been published and whether your class is enrolled.

## Face setup and camera

### The camera permission was denied

Open the browser's site settings for Easy2U, allow camera access, return to **Face Setup**, and select **Retry**. On a phone, also check the operating system camera permission for the browser.

### The camera is unavailable or busy

Close video calls and other apps/tabs using the camera. Check that the device has a working camera, then select **Retry**. If the browser says the page is not secure or cannot use the camera, open the institution's approved Easy2U address and contact the administrator if the issue continues.

### Face capture does not progress

Use a brighter, evenly lit place; position one face in the guide; remove obstructions; and follow the blink and head-turn instruction for each pose. Make sure no other person is in frame. Retry once the on-screen guidance indicates a centered face and suitable lighting.

### My enrollment says pending review

This is a normal review state, not an accusation. Wait for the lecturer or reviewer to complete the check, then revisit **Face Setup**. If an assessment is due before the review finishes, contact the lecturer and explain that your enrollment is pending. Do not attempt to bypass verification.

### Face setup succeeded, but the assessment still blocks me

Return to **Face Setup** and check its current status. A pending review or withdrawn biometric consent can block a face-required assessment. Complete the stated setup/review step. If your status is enrolled and the quiz still blocks you, contact the lecturer with the quiz title and exact message.

## Class quizzes and results

### The quiz says it is not open yet

Check the opening time shown on the quiz and try again after it begins. Confirm your device date/time is correct. If the displayed time has passed, ask the lecturer to verify the quiz schedule.

### The quiz says the window has closed

The availability period has ended. Contact the lecturer to ask whether the quiz can be reopened; students cannot change the window.

### I already attempted the quiz or cannot retake it

The attempt limit and retake setting are controlled by the lecturer. Return to the quiz card and use **Resume**, **Start**, or **View results** only when that action is shown. Ask the lecturer if you believe the attempt was interrupted or recorded incorrectly.

### My result says “Awaiting results”

The quiz was submitted but the lecturer has not released results. Check again later or ask when results will be available. You cannot access hidden scores from the student view before release.

### I cannot find “View results”

Confirm the lecturer has released results and that the quiz card shows the released state. Clear any class filter and verify you are in the correct class. If the quiz remains missing, contact the lecturer.

## Paused or interrupted assessment

### A pause overlay asks me to verify my face

Stay on the quiz page, center your face, and follow the blink instruction. Keep the camera available and wait for the recovery control/state. A pause is a recovery state; it does not by itself mean you have been accused of misconduct.

### The camera stopped during the assessment

Restore the Easy2U browser camera permission, close any other app using it, and use the retry/recovery action shown on the overlay. Keep the page open. Contact your lecturer if it does not resume.

### The app says I left the tab or fullscreen

Return to the quiz tab and accept the fullscreen prompt if shown. Keep the page in the foreground. If the app asks the lecturer to unlock the attempt, contact them with the quiz title and approximate pause time.

### The page reloaded, browser closed, or connection dropped

Reopen **Class Quizzes** and check whether the card offers a resume action. An answer is not guaranteed saved if the player did not acknowledge it before the interruption. Avoid starting a new attempt unless the card offers it or your lecturer advises you to.

### Submission failed or the timer expired

Keep the player open and use an on-screen retry if one appears. Wait for a completion/submission state, then check the quiz card. A timed attempt may submit automatically at expiry. If there is no completion state or the recorded status looks wrong, contact the lecturer promptly with the time and error text.

### A gesture or focus warning keeps appearing

Follow the on-screen instructions and keep your hand/face visible if those checks are enabled. Use a normal answer control only if the interface offers it. If you cannot operate the quiz, contact the lecturer; do not attempt to disable or bypass checks.

## Personal quizzes and sharing

### I cannot create or edit a personal quiz

Check required title, prompt, and answer fields, then retry the visible save/add action. AI generation and image options depend on institution configuration. If your quiz is missing from **My Quizzes** after a refresh, contact support using [Support](support.md).

### A shared quiz link says it is invalid or unavailable

Ask the owner for the current link. They may have rotated the code, stopped sharing, or deleted the quiz. A previous link will not work after rotation or revocation. Sign in if prompted, then reopen the current link.

### A shared quiz stopped working while I was playing

The owner may have stopped sharing during your attempt. Ask them for a new link if they intend to share it again. Answers or feedback may no longer be available after access is revoked.

## Classes and join codes

### I created a class, but students cannot join

Open **My Classes** and check that the class is active. From its detail page,
select **Copy join code** or **Show QR** and share the current code or link.
An archived class cannot accept new joins. If students see a temporary
lockout, they should wait for the displayed interval rather than keep trying
codes. Send them the [student join steps](student.md#join-a-class).

### A student is missing from the roster

Confirm that the student joined this class, not another class with a similar
name. On a phone, select the **Students** tab in class detail. If a large
class shows a truncation notice, only the first 100 students are listed, but
other enrolled students still count toward quizzes and exports. Check the
gradebook or contact [Support](support.md) if the enrollment is disputed.

## Quiz creation and publishing

### Publish quiz is unavailable

Open the draft in the builder and add at least one valid question. Check
each question's prompt, options or rubric, and answer key. A class must be
active to accept a new quiz. Review any validation message and retry the
visible **Publish quiz** action.

### Students cannot see or start a published quiz

Check that the status is **Live**, the student belongs to the class, and the
**Opens at** and **Closes at** times permit a start. A live quiz can be
scheduled for later or past its closing time. If the class is archived,
restore it only after checking whether its live quizzes should reopen.

### I closed a quiz by mistake

Closing is one-way. Do not promise students that it can be returned to Live.
Review the submitted attempts and results, then use your institution's
support route for the next course action. A duplicate creates a fresh draft,
but it does not carry student attempts or results.

## Assessment attempts and face checks

### A student is flagged or cannot resume

Open the quiz's **Results**, find the student's session, and read its status
and **Integrity timeline**. Decide whether **Unlock** is appropriate.
If a camera issue prevents checks, consider **Face-exempt** for that attempt
and record the required reason. Do not **Reset** unless you intend to
permanently delete that attempt's answers, progress and integrity details.

### Face enrollment needs review

On **My Classes**, inspect the pending enrollment panel. Use your normal
identity-check process before selecting **Approve** or **Reject — ask to
re-enroll**. A rejection asks the student to capture again. If you cannot
establish the identity, use [Support](support.md) rather than guessing.

### A result is missing or still hidden from students

Check that the student's attempt is completed. Inspect **Pending** or
**Needs review** short answers before releasing marks. On **Results**,
**Results are hidden** means students cannot yet see scores. Select
**Reveal to students** only when the assessment is ready. An **unrevealed**
marker in the gradebook is expected before release.

## AI import and export

### Import questions reports a line error

Return to **Bulk import questions**, check the line named in the message,
and correct its pipe-separated format and `*` answer marker. Confirm the
preview and that the draft has enough of its 30 question slots remaining.
Only `.txt` and `.csv` files load through this import dialog.

### AI generation or document scanning fails

Review the file type and size shown in **Generate quiz from file**. Check
the extracted-content preview before continuing. For scanned material,
switch to an available scanner or retry the pages the dialog names. If a
generation was cancelled, nothing was saved; if it says questions were
saved but the review screen failed, reopen the builder. You can add
questions manually while a configured AI service is unavailable.

### Export Excel fails or appears incomplete

Check whether you are on the quiz's **Results** page or the class
**Gradebook**; they produce different reports. Retry once if the page
reports a temporary export error. If a gradebook shows a row, column or
session truncation notice, read that notice before interpreting the file.
Store any downloaded report in an institution-approved location and use
[Support](support.md) if exports continue to fail.

## Phone layout

### I cannot see the bottom navigation

Close the on-screen keyboard. The bottom dock hides while a text field is
active so it does not cover the form. If you are taking an assessment, use
the quiz's own navigation controls; the usual app dock is absent in the
focused quiz player.

### The lecturer gradebook looks different on my phone

The desktop gradebook is a student-by-quiz table. On a phone it becomes a
student list with a sheet for one student or quiz at a time. Select a student
or quiz to inspect its details, then close the sheet to return to the list.

### A quiz-builder control is missing on my phone

Use the builder's add-question control to open its sheet, or expand a
question's compact controls. Some actions are under **More actions**.
If a control remains inaccessible after closing the keyboard, report the
device, browser, screen width and control name through [Support](support.md).

## Escalation

Contact your lecturer for class membership, quiz schedules, face review, attempt pauses/unlocks, retakes, and result release. Contact your institution's account administrator for sign-in, matric, institutional Microsoft sign-in, or camera/security configuration problems. If neither can resolve an app failure, use the student route in [Support](support.md). Include the exact message, quiz/class title, device/browser, and approximate time; never send your password, share code, or face image in a support report.
