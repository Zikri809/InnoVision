# During the quiz

Keep the quiz page open and follow the message shown in the player. A pause is a recovery state; it does not by itself mean your attempt has been flagged. Use this quick card if a check interrupts an assessment.

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Face check paused** or a blink prompt | Keep your face in the camera frame and blink when asked. Wait for the check to recover before answering. |
| **Hands left the camera** | Show your hand in the camera view, then blink to continue. |
| **Return to the exam** | Select **Return to the exam** to bring the quiz back to the foreground. Keep it open while answering. |
| Fullscreen was closed | Select **Return to the exam**. The quiz should continue where you left off. |
| Camera unavailable | Restore camera permission for Easy2U in your browser, close other apps using the camera, then follow the player prompt. |
| **Assessment flagged** or lecturer unlock required | Wait for your lecturer to review and unlock the attempt. Contact them with the quiz title and approximate time; do not start another attempt unless the quiz offers one. |
| Answer recording timed out or failed | Tap/click that answer again when the player asks you to retry. Check for the **Answer recorded** confirmation before moving on. |
| Submission timed out or failed | Keep the quiz page open and select **Retry submit**. Wait for the completion screen before leaving. |
| Timer reaches zero | The quiz submits automatically. Wait for the completion screen. |

For a face pause, the timed assessment countdown stops while the session is paused and resumes after recovery. Other quiz rules, including attempt limits and result release, are controlled by the lecturer.

If the page has reloaded or the connection has dropped, return to **Class Quizzes** and use the resume action if the quiz card offers one. Do not assume an answer was saved unless the player acknowledged it. For a pause that remains locked or a submission that still fails, contact your lecturer and use [Student troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md#paused-or-interrupted-assessment).
