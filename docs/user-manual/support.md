# Get more help

> **Internal edition:** use your existing class and institution IT channels.
> Add the actual helpdesk contact and lecturer communication channel before
> public distribution.

First, search [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md). If you are in an active
quiz, use [During a quiz: quick help](during-quiz.md).

## Students

Contact your lecturer through the class's usual communication channel when
the problem involves a join code, a missing class quiz, availability, an
assessment pause or flag, a result that has not been released, or permission
to retry. Your lecturer can inspect the attempt and decide whether an unlock,
face exemption, or reset is appropriate.

For account access, institutional Microsoft sign-in, or a matric-number
correction, use your institution's account or IT support channel. A lecturer
may refer you to that channel if the issue is outside the class.

## Lecturers

Use your institution's application or IT support channel for invite-code,
account, AI service, camera-service, or repeated export failures. Student
attempt actions remain in the quiz's Results page.

## What to include in a report

- Your role (student or lecturer), device type, browser, and whether you used
  English or Bahasa Melayu.
- The class or quiz title, approximate time, and the exact on-screen message.
- What you were trying to do and which documented recovery steps you tried.

Never send a password, lecturer invite code, matric number, face photo,
incident video, or a downloaded gradebook in an ordinary support message.
If a screenshot is requested, remove private information before sending it.
