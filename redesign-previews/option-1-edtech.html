Features

Everything you need for secure, modern assessment

Built for lecturers who want integrity without friction, and students who want a natural answering experience.

✋

Gesture Answering

Students answer MCQ and true/false questions by holding up fingers. MediaPipe hand tracking confirms selections with a hold-to-confirm gesture.

🛡️

Continuous Face Verification

Periodic identity checks with blink liveness detection ensure the enrolled student is the one taking the assessment — no invasive proctoring.

⚡

AI Quiz Generation

Upload lecture notes or documents and let the OCR + AI pipeline generate well-formed questions in seconds. Review and edit before publishing.

📊

Integrity Dashboard

A timeline of attendance, scores, and verification events. Unlock, exempt, or reset sessions with one click when issues arise.

🎯

Practice Mode

Unlimited attempts with immediate feedback help students learn the material before the graded assessment begins.

🔗

Simple Class Joining

Lecturers share a join code; students enroll their face once and they're ready for every quiz in that class.
