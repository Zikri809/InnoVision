The bot is already the character.
Let it work.

Today the dialog says the same thing three times — strip copy, five dots, and a churning console. These three concepts each collapse it to one living signal, built from the clay system and the morphing bot the app already has (thinking · scanning · success · fail · celebrate). Hit Run it on any stage, or jump to Done / Fail.

1

The Forge

One warm panel. Five clay coins drop into a slot as stages begin; the active coin ticks, finished coins turn green. The panel border itself warms toward green as work completes — the container is the progress bar. Errors brand the failing coin red and speak in the title line; nothing else moves.

The Takeover

While generating, the dialog becomes one big moment: the giant bot stands on its clay pedestal, orbit dots circling, and a single sentence underneath. The five stages are ticks on a chip rail — nothing else. On success the bot celebrates with a clay confetti burst and the CTA rises; on failure it droops and the reason gets one calm line.

3

Just the Bot

No status UI. The bot narrates: each stage is one speech bubble with a single evidence line; when the next stage starts, the bubble compresses into a tiny clay chip pinned above. History stays, tiny. On completion the last chip is the spark and the happy bot signs off — the quietest of the three.
